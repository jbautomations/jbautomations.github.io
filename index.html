How it works

Set up once.
Runs forever.

We handle the entire setup. You just add appointments to your calendar like you always have.

01
You add the appointment
Add your customer's booking to Google Calendar with their name and phone number. That's the only step you take.
02
The system detects it
Our automation checks your calendar every hour and spots any appointments coming up in the next 24 hours.
03
AI writes the reminder
Claude AI writes a personalised SMS for each customer — not a generic template, a real message with their name and details.
04
Customer gets the text
Your customer receives the reminder and replies YES to confirm, or calls you to reschedule. No more silent no shows.
